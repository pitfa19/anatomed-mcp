## What the finished paper establishes

Anatomed is a bounded 3D anatomy evidence layer and spatial-explanation protocol. It turns explicit anatomy requests into inspectable scenes while declining unresolved targets rather than inventing replacements.

Future implementation should preserve these commitments:

- anatomy comes from the authorized curated catalogue
- unresolved or ambiguous targets are declined rather than silently substituted
- context begins isolated and expands only when the question requires related or regional detail
- rendered focus and context remain bounded and inspectable
- the agent renders before explaining spatial anatomy
- explanations orient structures using anatomical axes
- visible evidence, separately cited knowledge, and unsupported inference remain distinct
